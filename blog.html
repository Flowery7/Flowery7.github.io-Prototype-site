Top Signs Your Roof Needs Attention (Before It's Too Late)

Your roof is your property's first line of defence against the British weather. Here are the warning signs to look out for so small problems don't become expensive ones.

Warning Signs to Watch

  • Missing or cracked tiles & slates — a clear sign of wear, especially after strong winds.
  • Damp patches or stains on ceilings or walls, often a sign of a small leak or slipped tile.
  • Sagging or dipping roof lines, which can indicate structural problems underneath.
  • Blocked or damaged gutters causing water to back up onto the roof and fascia.
  • Growing moss or plants between tiles can lift them and let water in.

Why Act Early?

Catching roof issues early is far cheaper than a full repair or re-roof. A quick, professional inspection can give you peace of mind and protect the value of your home.

Look closely at the eaves, verges, ridges, hips and valleys — these are where leaks most often start. Worn flashing and leadwork, cracked mortar on ridge tiles, damaged soffits and fascias, and tired felting, sarking or underlay beneath the tiles and slates can all let water in. Around a dormer or skylight, and where the roof pitch changes, are other common trouble spots.

If you've spotted any of these signs, contact Kestrel Property Solutions — we'll take a look and give you honest advice on the best way forward.

Planning the Perfect Garden Landscaping Project

Whether you want a low-maintenance garden, a family-friendly lawn or a patio perfect for summer entertaining, good planning makes all the difference.

Start With a Plan

  • Think about how you'll use the space — dining, playing, relaxing or entertaining.
  • Consider the practicalities — drainage, sunlight and access for materials.
  • Choose materials that suit your home and the local style for a cohesive look.
  • Budget for the whole project, including groundwork and finishing touches.

Why Work With Professionals?

Professional landscaping gets the groundwork right — proper drainage, solid bases and level surfaces that look great and last for years. It saves you time, avoids costly mistakes, and gives you a space you'll actually enjoy.

A well-built garden starts with the right subbase — usually aggregate or MOT Type 1 — topped with screeding before paving, kerbing and edging go down. We create hardstanding and retaining walls, install drainage, soakaway and ACO channels, and finish with turfing, topsoil, mulch and planting. Whether it's a patio, decking, fencing or trellis, getting the foundations right is everything.

At Kestrel Property Solutions we design and build gardens that suit your lifestyle and your budget.

How to Prepare a Room for a Professional Paint Job

A fresh coat of paint can completely transform a room. Good preparation is the secret to a smooth, long-lasting finish that looks professionally done.

Prepare Like a Pro

  • Move furniture and protect floors with dust sheets to avoid damage and clean-up stress.
  • Clean the walls to remove dust, grease and grime so the paint adheres properly.
  • Fill cracks and holes and sand smooth for an even surface.
  • Prime bare or patched surfaces to stop stains bleeding through and improve coverage.
  • Mask off edges for crisp lines around skirting, coving and windows.

The Professional Difference

Professional decorators bring the experience, tools and patience for a truly flawless finish. From exterior stonework to interior feature walls, Kestrel Property Solutions delivers painting you'll be proud of for years.

Good preparation means sanding, filler, caulk, knotting and a tack cloth before any paint goes on. We apply primer, undercoat and mist coat as a base, then emulsion on walls and gloss, eggshell or satinwood on woodwork — finished with clean cutting in and laying off. For decorative finishes we offer stippling, stencilling, scumbling, gilding, limewash and distemper.
