Who We Are

Kestrel Property Solutions was built on a simple idea: property owners shouldn't have to manage a dozen different tradesmen to keep their home or business in good shape.

We bring roofing, landscaping, gardening and painting together under one roof. That means one point of contact, one trusted team, and a quality finish on every job — whether it's fixing a roof, building a patio, tidying a garden or giving your walls a fresh new look.

We take pride in honest work, clear communication and fair prices. Every project, however big or small, is carried out to a standard we're happy to put our name to.

Based in the Home Counties, we cover Hertfordshire (including St Albans, Watford, Stevenage and Hemel Hempstead), Bedfordshire (including Luton, Bedford and Dunstable) and Buckinghamshire (including Aylesbury, High Wycombe and Milton Keynes). See the full list of areas we cover →

What services does Kestrel Property Solutions offer? +

We're a multi-service property company covering roofing, landscaping, gardening, and painting & decorating — for both residential and commercial clients. That means one trusted team for all your property needs.

Are you fully insured? +

Yes. All of our work is carried out by experienced, fully insured professionals to a high standard, giving you complete peace of mind on every job.

Do you handle both small repairs and large projects? +

Absolutely. From a single slipped tile or hedge trim to a full re-roof or complete garden redesign, every job gets the same care and attention — no project is too big or too small.
