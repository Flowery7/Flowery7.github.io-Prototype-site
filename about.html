Who We Are

Kestrel Property Solutions was built on a simple idea: property owners shouldn't have to manage a dozen different tradesmen to keep their home or business in good shape.

We bring roofing, landscaping, gardening and painting together under one roof. That means one point of contact, one trusted team, and a quality finish on every job — whether it's fixing a roof, building a patio, tidying a garden or giving your walls a fresh new look.

We take pride in honest work, clear communication and fair prices. Every project, however big or small, is carried out to a standard we're happy to put our name to.

On the roofing side, our team works across the whole roofline — from eaves, verges and guttering to soffits, fascias, ridges, hips and valleys. We replace battens, felting, sarking and underlay, fit new tiles and slates, install dormers and skylights, and carry out flashing, leadwork and mortar repointing at any roof pitch.

On the landscaping side, we build from the ground up — laying subbase, aggregate and MOT Type 1, screeding and paving, installing kerbing and edging, and creating hardstanding and retaining walls. We manage drainage, soakaway and ACO channels, lay turfing and topsoil, spread mulch, and build patios, decking, fencing and trellis — all finished with careful planting.

On the painting and decorating side, we prepare every surface properly — sanding, filler, caulk, knotting and a tack cloth before any paint goes on. We apply primer, undercoat, mist coat, emulsion, gloss, eggshell and satinwood, and finish with clean cutting in and laying off. For a special touch we offer stippling, stencilling, scumbling, gilding, limewash and distemper.

Based in the Home Counties, we cover Hertfordshire (including St Albans, Watford, Stevenage and Hemel Hempstead), Bedfordshire (including Luton, Bedford and Dunstable) and Buckinghamshire (including Aylesbury, High Wycombe and Milton Keynes). See the full list of areas we cover →

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Kestrel Property Solutions.

What services does Kestrel Property Solutions offer? +

We're a multi-service property company covering roofing, landscaping, gardening, and painting & decorating — for both residential and commercial clients. That means one trusted team for all your property needs.

Are you fully insured? +

Yes. All of our work is carried out by experienced, fully insured professionals to a high standard, giving you complete peace of mind on every job.

Do you handle both small repairs and large projects? +

Absolutely. From a single slipped tile or hedge trim to a full re-roof or complete garden redesign, every job gets the same care and attention — no project is too big or too small. That includes everything from eaves, guttering, fascias and soffits to battens, felting, flashing, leadwork, tiles, slates, dormers and skylights — from subbase, paving, kerbing and edging to turfing, topsoil, drainage, patios, decking, fencing and planting — and from primer, undercoat, mist coat and emulsion to gloss, eggshell, satinwood, cutting in and laying off.

How do I get a quote? +

Just call us on 0800 118 4172, email Kestrelproperty8@gmail.com, or fill in the quote form on our homepage. Quotes are always free and come with no obligation.

Which areas do you cover? +

We cover Hertfordshire (including St Albans, Watford, Stevenage and Hemel Hempstead), Bedfordshire (including Luton, Bedford and Dunstable) and Buckinghamshire (including Aylesbury, High Wycombe and Milton Keynes) for homes and businesses. See the full list of towns we cover → Not sure if we reach you? Give us a call — we're happy to help.

What are your opening hours? +

We're available Monday to Saturday, 08:00–18:00. For urgent enquiries, calling is the fastest way to reach us.

Do you guarantee your work? +

Yes. All of our roofing, landscaping, gardening and painting work is guaranteed, and we'll always come back if something isn't right. Many materials we fit — such as tiles, slates, felting and paving — also carry manufacturer warranties for extra peace of mind.

How long does a typical roofing job take? +

Most repairs — replacing slipped tiles, fixing flashing or clearing guttering — are done in a day. A full re-roof, including new battens, underlay and slates, usually takes three to five days depending on the size and pitch of the roof. We'll give you a clear timescale with your quote.

Do you supply the materials or do I need to buy them? +

We supply everything — from tiles, slates and leadwork to paving, decking, paint and plants. We only use trusted suppliers, and all materials are itemised in your quote so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

Will you tidy up after the job is finished? +

Always. We clear away all waste, sweep up and leave your property neat and tidy — whether we've been re-roofing, laying a patio or painting a room. Waste removal is included in every quote, with no hidden extras.

Can you help with emergency repairs? +

Yes. If storm damage has left your roof leaking or your property vulnerable, call us on 0800 118 4172 and we'll prioritise making it safe and watertight — then plan the permanent repair around you.
