A Complete Range of Property Services

🛠️ Roofing

Repairs, maintenance, re-roofing and gutter work. Protect your property from the elements with quality roofing services.

Info ▸
  • Flat & pitched roof repairs and full re-roofs
  • Gutter, fascia & soffit replacement and cleaning
  • Chimney repointing, leadwork & storm damage fixes
  • Tile, slate & felt roofing for homes and businesses

It's ideal for homeowners who've spotted a damp patch or missing tiles, landlords keeping rentals watertight, and businesses that can't afford a leaking roof disrupting trade. Booking is easy: just give us a call or send a quick message, we'll arrange a free survey at a time that suits you, and you'll get a clear, fixed quote with no obligation — most repairs can be scheduled within days.

🌳 Landscaping

Patios, driveways, turf, fencing and full garden redesigns. Transform your outdoor space into somewhere to be proud of.

Info ▸
  • Patios, pathways & block paving driveways
  • Turfing, artificial grass & garden levelling
  • Fencing, decking, sleepers & raised planters
  • Full garden design and hard landscaping builds

It's a great fit for families wanting a safe, usable outdoor space, new-build owners starting from a blank canvas, and businesses improving their kerb appeal. Getting started is simple: send us a photo or two of your garden, tell us roughly what you have in mind, and we'll come back with ideas and a free, itemised quote — most projects can be booked within a couple of weeks.

🌱 Gardening

Lawn care, hedge trimming, pruning, clearance and seasonal maintenance. Keep your garden healthy and tidy all year round.

Info ▸
  • Regular lawn mowing, feeding & weed treatment
  • Hedge cutting, tree pruning & shaping
  • Garden clearance, waste removal & jet washing
  • Seasonal planting, beds & borders maintenance

It's perfect for busy families, older customers who'd rather enjoy their garden than work in it, landlords maintaining tenant gardens, and holiday-let owners who need their outside space looking its best. Arranging a visit couldn't be easier — a quick call or message is all it takes, we'll agree a regular slot or one-off date that suits you, and there's no contract or commitment required.

🎨 Painting & Decorating

Interior and exterior painting with a crisp, professional finish. Refresh your home or give your business a new look.

Info ▸
  • Interior painting — walls, ceilings, woodwork
  • Exterior painting, masonry & render coatings
  • Wallpaper hanging & surface preparation
  • Commercial repaints & shopfront refreshes

It suits homeowners refreshing tired rooms, landlords between tenancies, and shop or office owners who want their premises to look welcoming. We offer flexible working around your schedule, including weekends for businesses that can't afford downtime — and booking is as simple as a phone call, with a free colour-consultation visit and a no-obligation written quote.
